Questions owners ask

What is A.T.F.O. (AI Tools For Owners)?

A.T.F.O., short for AI Tools For Owners, is a free website at aitoolsforowners.com that recommends AI tool stacks for small business owners. You type the task that takes up your time, and it shows which tools handle it, why each one is there, how long setup takes, and the steps to set it up. There is no signup.

What are the best AI tools for small business owners?

It depends on the job you want off your plate. These are our picks for the 16 tasks owners ask about most:

  • Following up with leads: HubSpot, Zapier and ChatGPT
  • Real estate lead follow up: Follow Up Boss, Grok Bot, Calendly and ChatGPT
  • Invoicing: QuickBooks Online, Zapier and Dext
  • Bookkeeping and expenses: Xero, Dext, Claude and Grok Bot
  • Scheduling appointments: Calendly, Zapier and Reclaim.ai
  • Social media posts: Canva, ChatGPT and Buffer
  • Customer support emails: Help Scout, Claude and Zapier
  • Answering website questions: Tidio, Intercom Fin and Zapier
  • Meeting notes: Fireflies.ai, Otter.ai and Zapier
  • Email newsletters: Mailchimp, ChatGPT and Canva
  • Online store customer service: Gorgias, Klaviyo and ChatGPT
  • Proposals and contracts: PandaDoc, Claude and Zapier
  • Getting customer reviews: Birdeye, Zapier and ChatGPT
  • Client intake and onboarding: Typeform, Zapier and Loom
  • Writing blog posts and marketing copy: Claude, Grammarly and Canva
  • Payroll: Gusto, QuickBooks Online and Zapier

Should my small business use Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok?

Pick the assistant that fits how you already work. Here is what each one is best for:

  • Claude: Careful writing, long documents, and replies that sound like you
  • Google Gemini: Owners who already run on Gmail, Docs, Sheets and Calendar
  • Grok Bot: Recurring work across email, CRMs and websites that you want done on a schedule
  • ChatGPT: A general all-rounder, and the one most people already know
  • Full comparison: aitoolsforowners.com/compare-ai-assistants-for-small-business/

How long does it take to set up an AI tool stack?

Most stacks take between 25 min and 2 hr 25 min of setup time in total. Every tool is marked Easy or Medium, and each stack comes with a short setup checklist in plain English.

Is AIToolsForOwners free?

Yes. The tool finder, task pages and guides are free, and you don't need an account. Some links are affiliate links, which may earn us a commission at no cost to you.

Do the recommended AI tools cost money?

Many of the tools have free plans you can start on. Each task page lists current plans and starting prices, so you can check costs before you sign up for anything.
