AI Tools For Owners: black and white logos
==========================================

Same lettering as the main logo: the first letter of each word (A, T, F, O) is heavy
and the rest is light, so the name also reads as ATFO.

Files (SVG scales to any size; PNG is large with a transparent background)
- atfo-bw-stacked-black          Black logo for light backgrounds. Main version.
- atfo-bw-stacked-white          White logo for dark backgrounds.
- atfo-bw-stacked-twotone        Heavy letters black, the rest gray: ATFO stands out most.
- atfo-bw-stacked-twotone-white  Two-tone for dark backgrounds.
- atfo-bw-stacked-card-white     Black logo on a white rounded card (social posts, print).
- atfo-bw-stacked-card-black     White logo on a black rounded card.
- atfo-bw-horizontal-black / -white / -twotone   One-line versions for headers and email.
- atfo-bw-icon-black / -white    The check-mark icon on its own (profile pictures, favicons).

Colors: black #0E0E0E, white #FFFFFF, gray #6E6E6E (light gray #A8A8A8 on dark).
