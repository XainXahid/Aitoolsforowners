Lead follow-up

Stop losing leads you already paid for.

Most leads don't say no. They just never hear back fast enough.

This week's stack catches every form fill, sends a friendly follow-up on its own, and drafts the personal replies for you. Connecting the tools takes about an hour.

The stack

HubSpot ↗ 30 min setup · Easy

Holds every lead and sends follow-up sequences.

Zapier ↗ 20 min setup · Easy

Pushes new leads from forms into HubSpot.

ChatGPT ↗ 5 min setup · Easy

Drafts personal follow-up emails in seconds.

Time to connect the tools: about an hour

Set it up

1 Create a free HubSpot account and import your lead list.
2 Connect your web form to HubSpot with a Zapier zap.
3 Write a 3 email follow up sequence with ChatGPT, then load it into HubSpot.

Steal this prompt

You're my sales assistant. Write a friendly 3-email follow up for a lead who asked about [service]. Email 1 goes out today, email 2 in 3 days, email 3 in a week. Keep each under 90 words, sound like a real person, and end each with one easy question.

Works in Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini or Grok

Quick win: reply within minutes

Leads go cold fast. Even a one-line "Got your message, I'll call you at 3" keeps the conversation going.
